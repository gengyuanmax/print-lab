元素实验室Element Lab元素实验室Element Lab

网页组件、图形和照片怎样融入瑞士风格、Riso 和半色调设计？逐项调整配色、线条和网点，对照改造前后的变化；也可处理单张照片，或比较两个元素的叠印、挖空等组合效果。各项说明附有参考来源。When an element feels out of place in a Swiss / Riso / halftone design, it usually has not gone through the same print pipeline. This lab translates one element step by step: switch each step on or off, compare before and after on the same paper, and check what still does not fit and where each rule comes from.
