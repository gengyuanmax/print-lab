从瑞士风格的版式、Riso 孔版印刷到半色调网点，通过交互实验了解设计与印刷。四个实验室分别探索图像的印刷质感、图文编排、纸张的折叠与装订，以及网页组件的风格改造。Interactive experiments with Swiss, Riso and halftone print styles. Each lab breaks one part of the look into settings you can adjust: how an image becomes ink, how a page is laid out, and how paper becomes an object you hold.

四个实验室各看什么How the labs divide the work

一种视觉风格往往由设计、工艺和材料共同形成。预设先展示效果，再说明网点、配色、错位或纸纹各自从何而来，方便你逐项调整、比较。A “style” here is a set of final visual features you recognise at a glance, not a particular production process. So every preset describes the final look, then marks whether each feature comes from a design decision, a process or a material.

模拟管线The simulation pipeline

四个实验室共用一条管线：先把元素排进版面，再拆成每种墨一块的灰度版，加网，按透明油墨叠印到纸上，最后裁切、折叠成实物。下面六张图是同一张小样走完这条管线的每一步，都是页面现场算出来的；每一步下方对照真实的 Riso 印刷在做什么，以及模拟和它差在哪里。All four labs share one pipeline: arrange the elements on the page, split it into one greyscale plate per ink, screen each plate, overprint the translucent inks on paper, then trim and fold it into an object. The six images below are one small proof going through each step, computed live on this page; under each step is what a real Riso print does at that point, and where the simulation differs.

03分版Plates

每种墨一张灰度图：A 版走亮部和中间调，B 版走暗部和文字。One greyscale image per ink: plate A takes the lights and midtones, plate B the darks and the type.

真实印刷On press每种颜色导出一份 300 dpi 的灰度文件，按颜色命名；每层墨都要有自己的一张灰度图来制版。Export one 300 dpi greyscale file per colour, named after its ink; each ink layer needs its own greyscale image to make a master.模拟的差别：这里按亮度自动分配两块版；实际是设计师自己决定每块版上放什么。Where the simulation differs: plates are split automatically by lightness here; in practice the designer decides what goes on each plate.Good Press · MICA

04加网Screen

深浅变成网点：A 版 15°，B 版 75°，墨越多点越大。Tone becomes dots: plate A at 15°, B at 75°; more ink, bigger dots.

真实印刷On press加网在制版时由机器完成（600 dpi）：可选 Grain Touch 随机扩散抖动，或 Screen 细小网点，网线频率可调。热敏头在版纸（master）上烧出微孔。Screening happens in the machine as the master is made (600 dpi): Grain Touch, a random diffusion dither, or Screen, fine halftone dots with adjustable frequency. A thermal head burns microscopic holes into the master.模拟的差别：这里用规则的大圆点，方便在屏幕上看清；机器的网点小得多，Grain 模式更像颗粒。Where the simulation differs: dots here are regular and large so they read on screen; the machine's are far smaller, and Grain looks more like film grain.CCS · Risograph

05印刷Print

透明油墨相乘叠到纸上，B 版错开一点，再加颗粒和缺墨。Translucent inks multiply on paper, plate B slips a little, then grain and starved ink.

真实印刷On press版包在滚筒上，油墨从微孔压到纸上；一种颜色一个滚筒，纸每种颜色走一遍。套色无法避免地差 1–2 mm。大豆油墨需要非涂布纸才干得了。The master wraps a drum and ink is forced through its holes onto the paper; one drum per colour, one pass per colour. Colours miss by 1–2 mm, unavoidably. Soy-based ink needs uncoated paper to dry.模拟的差别：错位在这里只是整体平移，颜色按相乘混合；实际会有倾斜、拉伸，墨量和印刷顺序也会改变混色。Where the simulation differs: misregistration is a plain shift here and colours multiply; on press it also skews and stretches, and ink density and print order change the mix.Risograph · Oxford Greenprint
0102

06成品Object

带出血印在大纸上，沿裁切线裁下，再沿折线对折。Printed with bleed on a larger sheet, cut at the trim, folded at the crease.

真实印刷On press墨干后用切纸机沿裁切标记裁掉出血，再折页；多页的要先拼版，再骑马钉装订。Once the ink is dry, a guillotine trims the bleed at the crop marks, then the sheet is folded; multi-page work is imposed first, then saddle-stitched.模拟的差别：这里只画出示意，裁切和折页的误差没有模拟。Where the simulation differs: only a diagram here; cutting and folding tolerances are not simulated.Good Press · Imposition
