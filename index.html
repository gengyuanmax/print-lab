从瑞士风格的版式、Riso 孔版印刷到半色调网点，通过交互实验了解设计与印刷。四个实验室分别探索图像的印刷质感、图文编排、纸张的折叠与装订，以及网页组件的风格改造。Interactive experiments with Swiss, Riso and halftone print styles. Each lab breaks one part of the look into settings you can adjust: how an image becomes ink, how a page is laid out, and how paper becomes an object you hold.

四个实验室各看什么How the labs divide the work

一种视觉风格往往由设计、工艺和材料共同形成。预设先展示效果，再说明网点、配色、错位或纸纹各自从何而来，方便你逐项调整、比较。A “style” here is a set of final visual features you recognise at a glance, not a particular production process. So every preset describes the final look, then marks whether each feature comes from a design decision, a process or a material.

从原稿到成品The simulation pipeline

从设计到印刷成品，通常要经过原稿准备、排版、分色、加网、印刷和印后加工。下面以一张双色小样演示这六个阶段，图像由页面实时生成。每一步都附有实际 Riso 制作流程的说明，并指出本页模拟做了哪些简化；编号链接可进入相应实验室。All four labs share one pipeline: arrange the elements on the page, split it into one greyscale plate per ink, screen each plate, overprint the translucent inks on paper, then trim and fold it into an object. The six images below are one small proof going through each step, computed live on this page; under each step is what a real Riso print does at that point, and where the simulation differs.

03分版Plates

每种油墨对应一张灰度分色稿。本例用 A 版表现亮部与中间调，B 版表现暗部和文字。One greyscale image per ink: plate A takes the lights and midtones, plate B the darks and the type.

实际制作On press每种油墨分别准备灰度分色稿，用于制作对应的版纸。Good Press 要求文件为 300 dpi，并在文件名中注明油墨颜色。Export one 300 dpi greyscale file per colour, named after its ink; each ink layer needs its own greyscale image to make a master.本页简化：按亮度自动分成两版；实际制作中，各色版的内容由设计师根据效果安排。Where the simulation differs: plates are split automatically by lightness here; in practice the designer decides what goes on each plate.Good Press · MICA

04加网Screen

用网点大小表现明暗。本例 A 版网角为 15°，B 版为 75°，颜色越深，网点越大。Tone becomes dots: plate A at 15°, B at 75°; more ink, bigger dots.

实际制作On press设备在制版时将图像转换为微小的印刷点，再由热敏头在版纸（master）上形成微孔。加网模式、网线数和制版分辨率取决于设备与设置。Screening happens in the machine as the master is made (600 dpi): Grain Touch, a random diffusion dither, or Screen, fine halftone dots with adjustable frequency. A thermal head burns microscopic holes into the master.本页简化：用放大的规则圆点方便观察；实际印刷的网点通常更细，随机加网则呈现颗粒状效果。Where the simulation differs: dots here are regular and large so they read on screen; the machine's are far smaller, and Grain looks more like film grain.CCS · Risograph

05印刷Print

用正片叠底模拟油墨叠印，将 B 版稍作偏移，再加入颗粒和缺墨效果。Translucent inks multiply on paper, plate B slips a little, then grain and starved ink.

实际制作On press版纸包覆在油墨滚筒上，油墨穿过微孔印到纸面，各色分别制版后套印。Riso 通常使用吸墨性较好的非涂布纸；Oxford Greenprint 提醒设计时考虑约 1–2 mm 的套色偏差。The master wraps a drum and ink is forced through its holes onto the paper; one drum per colour, one pass per colour. Colours miss by 1–2 mm, unavoidably. Soy-based ink needs uncoated paper to dry.本页简化：套色偏差只用整体平移表现，混色采用正片叠底。实际还可能出现倾斜或伸缩，油墨特性、墨量和印刷顺序也会影响颜色。Where the simulation differs: misregistration is a plain shift here and colours multiply; on press it also skews and stretches, and ink density and print order change the mix.Risograph · Oxford Greenprint

06成品Object

在较大的纸张上印刷并预留出血，按裁切线裁到成品尺寸，再沿折线折叠。Printed with bleed on a larger sheet, cut at the trim, folded at the crease.

实际制作On press待油墨干燥后，按裁切标记裁去多余部分，再进行折页或装订。小册子需在印刷前按装订方式拼版，骑马钉是其中一种常见方式。Once the ink is dry, a guillotine trims the bleed at the crop marks, then the sheet is folded; multi-page work is imposed first, then saddle-stitched.本页简化：仅展示裁切与折页示意，未模拟加工误差。Where the simulation differs: only a diagram here; cutting and folding tolerances are not simulated.Good Press · Imposition

算法说明：噪声、影调与加网Algorithms: noise fields, fitted curves, screening

p 表示以 CSS 像素计的页面坐标。噪声按这个坐标取样，使纹理尺寸不随屏幕像素密度改变。下列系数按视觉效果调整，未经实物印样测量或校准。p is the position in CSS pixels of the page, not device pixels, so a given p gives the same noise on every screen. The coefficients below were fitted by eye, not measured from prints.
