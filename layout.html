把 Swiss、半调海报和 Riso zine 的版式拆成参数：网格、字号比例、破格强度、留白、形状、引导线和印刷标记。换一个随机种子，同一套规则会生成另一张海报。The layouts of Swiss posters, halftone posters and Riso zines, broken into parameters: grid, type scale, rule-breaking, whitespace, shapes, guides and print marks. Change the seed and the same rules produce another poster.

指标按元素外框近似计算：留白率 = 未被元素覆盖的面积；对齐度 = 左边缘落在栏线上的比例；重心以页面中心为 50% / 50%。Metrics are approximated from element bounding boxes: whitespace is the area no element covers; alignment is the share of left edges that land on column lines; balance puts the page centre at 50% / 50%.
