指标针对第 1 页、按元素外框近似计算：留白率 = 未被元素覆盖的面积；对齐度 = 左边缘落在栏线上的比例；重心以页面中心为 50 / 50；对比度按正文的实际颜色计算。Metrics describe page 1 and are approximated from element bounding boxes: whitespace is the area no element covers; alignment is the share of left edges on column lines; balance puts the page centre at 50 / 50; contrast uses the body text's actual colour.
