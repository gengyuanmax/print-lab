光照、温度及纸张成分会影响老化；含木质素的酸性纸较容易泛黄、变脆（Acid-free paper）；旧纸上的褐斑（foxing）可能与微生物、金属杂质和湿度等因素有关，不能一概视为霉斑（Foxing）。本页还可模拟折痕处墨层开裂、露出纸色的效果。Acidic paper that still contains lignin slowly yellows and turns brittle under light and heat (Acid-free paper); the brown spots on old paper are called foxing, and their cause is still debated: fungus, oxidising metals in the pulp, humidity (Foxing). 瑕疵Misprint MISPRINT瑕疵

网点扩大使墨点增大、边缘变粗，中间调的变化尤其明显。RGB 通道偏移则将红、蓝通道向两侧错开，模拟数字故障效果。“活字磨损”仅在凸版模式下生效：模拟字形边缘缺口、色块中的木纹和局部墨色不匀，并加强压痕效果。Dot gain fattens the dots and roughens their edges, most visibly in the midtones. RGB split is misregistration on a screen: the red and blue channels slide apart for a glitch look. Type wear only affects letterpress: chipped edges, wood grain in the solids, each block inked a little differently and a deeper impression.
