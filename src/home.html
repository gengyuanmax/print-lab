三个实验室怎么分工How the labs divide the work

这里说的“风格”，是一组能一眼认出来的最终视觉特征，而不是某一条生产流程。所以每个预设都描述最终效果，再标出每个特征来自设计决定、工艺还是材料。A “style” here is a set of final visual features you recognise at a glance, not a particular production process. So every preset describes the final look, then marks whether each feature comes from a design decision, a process or a material.
