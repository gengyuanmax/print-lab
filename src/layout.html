以下指标仅统计第 1 页。留白率按未被元素外框覆盖的面积估算；对齐度表示左边缘与栏线对齐的元素占比；重心坐标以页面中心为 50 / 50；正文对比度根据文字实际用色计算。Metrics describe page 1 and are approximated from element bounding boxes: whitespace is the area no element covers; alignment is the share of left edges on column lines; balance puts the page centre at 50 / 50; contrast uses the body text's actual colour.
